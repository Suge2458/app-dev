# Need for Speed
> one of my favorite movies, because every race feels fast and intense.
## About the Movie
**Need for Speed** was release in 2014 and its based on the popular video game franchise of the same name. It follows **Tobey Marshall**, a street racer and mechanic who is framed for a crime he didn't commit. After getting out of prison, he joins a race to settle the score with his rival.
